Three harnesses: A supply, B switching, C low voltage. Color drawings · 28-ID schedule · 56-end list · Measurement worksheet.

Known limitation: do not copy the neutral branches at Finder K5 terminal 3 and K1 terminal 3. Finder rates each 22.32 contact terminal for one 6 mm² (10 AWG) or two 4 mm² (12 AWG) conductors. Four 14 AWG wires at K5 terminal 3 (about 8.3 mm²) and three at K1 terminal 3 (about 6.3 mm²) exceed that rating. The reference enclosure is built this way. A new build should branch neutral on a rated DIN-rail neutral terminal block or other listed distribution so that each Finder terminal stays within its rating, and have the change checked by a qualified electrician.

Checkout after assembly. Set the Mean Well MDR-20-5 supply to 5 V and measure 5.00 V DC at its output with all relays closed. Confirm Home Assistant/RS485 communication, outlet sensing, a normal timed run, STOP and flow-loss shutdown, then calibrate with water using pump calibration. Wiring completion does not replace these measurements.

1. Metal box and existing equipment

The source-power whip and pump-outlet whip each contain stranded 14 AWG black hot, white neutral and green ground and terminate in the metal box. Their neutrals join there. Their grounds are joined on a ground Wago and bonded to the box. Neutral and protective ground remain separate. The source-whip black hot and outlet-whip black hot connect to separate Wagos.

Exactly four stranded 14 AWG harness wires connect those Wagos to the controls: A01 black OUT to F1 bottom LINE; A08 white OUT to Finder K5 power terminal 3; B04 red IN to the outlet-whip black hot; A17 green OUT from the ground Wago to the Mean Well FG terminal. Mean Well requires the MDR-20 FG terminal to be connected to protective earth. Follow the installed Wago conductor/strip instructions; no ferrules are specified at these four box ends. Keep the four harness leads together through the suitable protected entry.

F1 top LOAD supplies the four black branches below. Schneider permits reverse feeding of the C60. Retain upstream protection appropriate to the source whip, GFCI and disconnect. Preserve the device order in the reference photo: W1 Master, K1 Finder, W2 Slave, K5 Finder, K4 detector, PS1 Mean Well MDR-20-5, F1 breaker. The reference photo predates the swap from the HDR-15-5. Match molded terminal labels. PS1 stays upright with AC terminals down, at least 5 mm free on each side, 40 mm above and 20 mm below. Existing devices total 128.3 mm; including PS1 side gaps, reserve 138.3 mm before end stops/routing. Measure actual clearances on the installed rail.

Finder operating power and front indicators

The installed model is 22.32.0.120.1320. Finder's installation instructions and Series 22 catalog identify A1/A2 as the coil supply, 1–2 and 3–4 as separate normally open power contacts. A1 receives switched hot from its own controller; A2 receives continuous neutral through A15 at K1 and A14 at K5. Both A1 and A2 stay single blue.

Coil power operates both contact pairs. The LED follows coil supply and the mechanical window indicates operation; neither needs an extra wire or proves pump-outlet voltage. The 1320 model has no Auto-On-Off selector. Finder's catalog covers stranded/ferruled conductors; its general guide, page XVI, covers stranded and bootlace-ferrule conductors in box clamps. The installation sheet's separate solid-only note is labeled for field-wiring terminals; whether it applies to these internal chassis runs has not been established. The same sheet lists UL installation conditions for spacing, protection and surges.

Master flow-switch harness

Join yellow GPIO1 to the telephone cord's red conductor and black GND to green, then finish with an outer heat-shrink. The cord's RJ11 plug connects through the RJ11-to-RJ11 coupling at the enclosure bottom to the generic Hayward-style flow switch. Keep the connector's contact mapping consistent end to end; no numbered RJ11 pinout is specified.

The Master uses its configured internal pull-up. Its red 3.3 V and orange GPIO2 leads remain individually insulated and unused. No external resistor is added to this flow-switch tail.

Slave pull-up — local three-conductor splice

  1. With power disconnected and the GPIO plug unplugged, join the red 3.3 V factory lead directly to either lead of R1 at JR1. R1 is the specified 270 Ω, ¼ W resistor and has no polarity.
  2. At JS2, join exactly these three conductors: yellow GPIO1 factory lead + R1's other lead + orange C13. That resistor lead is labeled C12; it is part of R1, not an extra piece of hookup wire.
  3. Route orange C13 alone to K4 terminal 14, using a single 18 AWG ferrule at that device end.
  4. Join the black GND factory lead to black C14 at JS3. Route C14 alone to K4 terminal 11, also with a single 18 AWG ferrule.
  5. Leave K4 terminal 12 unused. Individually insulate the thin orange GPIO2 plug lead.

The connection arrangement follows the intended pull-up circuit: red 3.3 V → R1 → yellow GPIO1/orange-output node. K4 closes that node to black GND through 14–11 when the outlet is powered.

C11 and C15 are retired. The brown extension and second orange run are removed from the schedule. There is no shared ferrule at K4-14; the branch is the local JS2 solder splice.

Splice finishing reference

Use the factory-crimped four-position SH1.0 pigtail, the existing R1, and orange/black 18 AWG detector wires. The Master flow tail uses telephone cord and an RJ11 coupling. Factory leads and telephone cord keep their own gauge; do not replace their tiny connector contacts with 18 AWG wire.

  1. Slide suitable heat-shrink onto the wires before making the joints. Make each solder connection mechanically engaged and fully wetted; remove protruding strand ends. Use electronics-grade rosin flux rather than plumbing flux.
  2. Individually cover the red/R1 splice, the three-conductor JS2 splice, and the black return splice. Insulation must extend onto intact wire insulation and leave no bare resistor leads. Keep the red 3.3 V side insulated from JS2, then add an outer sleeve over the supported assembly.
  3. Individually cap unused plug leads. Support the pigtail and detector wires with a relaxed service loop so the connector and soldered joints do not carry cable pull.
  4. Keep this GPIO assembly in the low-voltage route. K4 A1/A2 retain their separate 14 AWG sensing connections; GPIO wires connect only to K4 11/14.

6. Harness and system checkout

Perform these checks with the Stenner unplugged. Record each measured result; a completed procedure is not a measurement.

With electronics disconnected, check each wire and shared group against the schedule. Continuity through each branch comes from its scheduled wires. Confirm BOX N remains connected to PS1 N, both Finder A2s and K4 A2 regardless of either contactor state; no required neutral path uses the 3–4 switching contacts. Check hot/neutral, the independent switched-coil leads, all low-voltage nets and exposed metal remain correctly separated. Follow the actual terminal/ferrule instructions for insertion, retention and torque. Restore the listed metal-box connections without adding a neutral-ground bond.

With power disconnected, verify W1 switches only K1's coil, W2 only K5's coil, and the motor path is F1 → K1 1–2 → K5 1–2 → B04/J1. K4 senses the output after both contacts. The onboard relay contacts switch only coils; the F1 shared hot termination must support the protected feed. Do not treat coil/resistor resistance as an insulation fault or apply insulation-test voltage to connected electronics. Record the measured checkout results with the assembly notes.
