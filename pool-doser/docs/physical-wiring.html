Four box wires.

Four stranded 14 AWG wires connect the controls to Wagos inside the metal box: black hot, white neutral, red switched return and green ground to the Mean Well FG terminal. The source-power and pump-outlet whips terminate in that box. Select any wire to see both ends.

Warning. Known limitation: do not copy the neutral branches at Finder K5 terminal 3 and K1 terminal 3. Finder rates each 22.32 contact terminal for one 6 mm² (10 AWG) or two 4 mm² (12 AWG) conductors. Four 14 AWG wires at K5-3 (about 8.3 mm²) and three at K1-3 (about 6.3 mm²) exceed that rating. The reference enclosure is built this way. A new build should branch neutral on a rated DIN-rail neutral terminal block or other listed distribution so that each Finder terminal stays within its rating, and have the change checked by a qualified electrician.

Checkout. Set the Mean Well MDR-20-5 supply to 5 V. After assembly, measure 5.00 V at the DC supply output with all relays closed, then confirm Home Assistant/RS485 communication, outlet sensing, a normal timed run, STOP and flow-loss shutdown. Calibrate with water before chemical service; see pump-calibration.md. Completed wiring does not replace these measurements. Assembly and checkout · Pump calibration.

One ferrule at the branch, single blue ends at small AC terminals

Protected hot: F1 top LOAD holds one installed large-red ferrule with four conductors: A02 → Mean Well L, A05 → W2 COM, A06 → K1 terminal 1 and A07 → W1 COM. All four leads are black 14 AWG. Each destination gets one blue ferrule. A01 still supplies bottom LINE.

Neutral: white A08 enters Finder K5 power terminal 3. Its installed large-red ferrule also contains A11 to K1 terminal 3, A13 to Mean Well N and A14 to K5 A2. K1 terminal 3 holds A11, A15 to K1 A2 and A16 to detector K4 A2 in another large-red ferrule. All neutral wires stay 14 AWG; all four neutral loads receive single blue ends. Both terminal-3 groups exceed Finder's rated terminal capacity; see the warning above.

Finder A1/A2 stay single: B01/B02 supply A1 from the respective Waveshare NO. A15/A14 supply the two A2 neutrals. Terminal 3 is distinct from A1/A2. Both terminal 4s have no external wire; they become connected to neutral when their 3–4 contacts close. No required neutral path goes through a switching contact. Pump hot still passes through K1 1–2 and K5 1–2 in series.

Ferrule dimensions from the kit chart (Pittsburgh 70195): blue 14 AWG: 2.6 mm metal-tube OD / 8 mm barrel; yellow 10 AWG: 3.9 mm / 12 mm; large red 8 AWG: 4.9 mm / 12 mm. Those ODs do not specify the finished crimp dimensions. Large red is used at both four-wire ends and the three-wire K1-3 end. Yellow is used at K5-2 for B04+B05. Sheet 08 shows each group.

Low voltage: the red +5 V and black DC-return chains remain PS1 → W2 → W1 using 18 AWG. Their twins at W2 must fit the blue-sized terminal envelope. VCC receives only 5 V DC; AC neutral, DC return and protective ground stay separate. The Master flow tail now records the installed telephone cord and RJ11 coupling.

Sheet 07 — photographed GPIO harnesses: plug colors are yellow GPIO1, unused orange GPIO2, red 3.3 V and black GND. On the Master, yellow joins red telephone C09 and black joins green telephone C10; the finished RJ11 tail is heat-shrunk. On the Slave, red joins R1 directly; its other lead joins yellow GPIO1 and orange C13 at the local JS2 splice. Orange C13 alone goes to K4-14; black C14 alone goes to K4-11. Insulate each joint separately. C11/C15 are retired. GPIO instructions.

Four box leads: A01 black OUT, A08 white OUT, B04 red IN and A17 green OUT from the ground Wago to the Mean Well FG terminal, which Mean Well requires to be connected to protective earth. The two black/white/green 14 AWG whips and their Wagos stay in the metal box. No external distribution devices are added. Grounds remain bonded there; neutral and ground remain separate.

Assembly and testing: After assembly, measure 5.00 V at the DC supply output with all relays closed, then confirm Home Assistant/RS485 communication, outlet sensing, a normal timed run, STOP and flow-loss shutdown. Calibrate with water before chemical service; see pump-calibration.md. Completed wiring does not replace these measurements. See the assembly instructions.
